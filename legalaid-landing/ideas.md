# LegalAid landing page ideas

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Briefing Room
Very Brief Intro: A warm editorial legal-research direction: cream paper, ink, and precise annotation marks. It makes complex AI review feel calm, credible, and legible.
Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Signal / Noise
Very Brief Intro: A high-contrast forensic interface where redactions, evidence trails, and chartreuse signal markers turn legal uncertainty into something visible. It feels like a research instrument rather than a generic SaaS page.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Civic Modernist
Very Brief Intro: A restrained public-interest identity with cobalt, white space, and modular typography. It communicates access, trust, and accountability with a clean institutional edge.
Probability: 0.09

## Chosen direction: Signal / Noise

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial modernism fused with forensic data visualization: part legal brief, part research lab, part art-directed instrument panel.

### Core Principles
1. Make evidence visible: every claim should feel connected to a source, clause, or verification state.
2. Create productive tension: generous paper-like space is interrupted by sharp signal colors, redactions, and diagnostic marks.
3. Prefer asymmetry over generic SaaS symmetry: let the page read like a curated investigation, not a centered template.
4. Keep the human in control: AI is framed as a set of inspectable instruments, never as an oracle.

### Color Philosophy
Ink-black (#101412) is the ground of seriousness and focus. Bone (#F1EEE6) carries the tactile authority of a legal page. Electric chartreuse (#D7FF52) is the ownable signal color: it marks verified movement and the moment a hidden clause becomes legible. Cobalt (#3158FF) carries retrieval and reasoning. Signal red (#F06455) is reserved for risk and exceptions, never decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
An offset editorial composition: a narrow reading rail, oversized type that sits left of the main visual, and cards that behave like pinned evidence rather than a uniform grid. Sections alternate between dark workbench surfaces and light paper surfaces, with thin ruled connectors creating continuity.

### Signature Elements
1. Bracket marks and clause ticks that echo legal redlines.
2. Thin cobalt evidence trails linking a finding to its source.
3. Chartreuse verification dots and small uppercase status labels.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like inspecting a document: hover reveals a little more context, buttons compress on press, and navigation moves the reader to the relevant section rather than opening dead-end placeholders. The key CTA should feel like opening the case file, not starting a sales funnel.

### Animation
Use short, high-confidence reveals: fade and translate up in 180–260ms, staggered by 40ms. Evidence trails can draw in once on first view, while signal dots use a slow 4s pulse. Avoid springy or playful motion. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep all motion to opacity and transform.

### Typography System
Display: Space Grotesk, 700–500, with tight tracking for headlines and compact uppercase labels. Body: IBM Plex Sans, 400–500, for readable technical prose. Mono accents: IBM Plex Mono for evidence states, metadata, and the occasional clause reference.

### Brand Essence
LegalAid is an explainable legal stress-testing framework for teams who need to find what a document is trying not to say. Personality: forensic, lucid, unflinching.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, specific, and slightly adversarial. CTAs sound like actions in a review workflow, not generic growth copy.

Example lines:
- “Find the clause that changes the deal.”
- “Open the evidence trail.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a bracketed evidence node: a sharp open bracket containing one central verification point and three small agent points. The wordmark is set in a custom-tight Space Grotesk treatment with a split-stem “A” feel; the symbol stays distinct so it can work as a favicon and system stamp.

### Signature Brand Color
Electric chartreuse #D7FF52 — a luminous, ownable signal against ink-black and bone.
