MASTER SERVICES AND SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 1, 2026, by and between Apex Enterprise Solutions, Inc. ("Customer") and OmniCloud Tech, LLC ("Vendor").

1. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES
Vendor shall provide cloud infrastructure and custom integration services as specified in Statements of Work. Customer shall accept deliverables upon delivery without right of formal acceptance testing.

2. INDEMNIFICATION AND EXPOSURE
Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer, its parent companies, affiliates, directors, and agents from and against any and all claims, liabilities, losses, damages, costs, and reasonable attorney's fees arising out of or resulting from: (a) any third-party claim alleging intellectual property infringement; (b) any bodily injury or tangible property damage; or (c) any operational failure or security incident involving Vendor's services, whether direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential, without limitation.

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Except for indemnification obligations under Section 2, breach of confidentiality obligations, or intentional misconduct, neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall exceed the total fees paid by Customer to Vendor in the three (3) months preceding the claim. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Customer's liability cap shall not apply to payment obligations or any claims of unauthorized usage.

4. TERMINATION AND SUSPENSION
Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience upon thirty (30) days written notice. Vendor may terminate immediately upon written notice if Customer fails to pay any undisputed invoice within ten (10) days of due date. Upon termination, all licenses granted to Customer shall immediately terminate, and Vendor shall delete all Customer data after forty-eight (48) hours unless an extended transition period is mutually agreed upon in writing.

5. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS AND EXCLUSIVITY
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of eighteen (18) months following termination for any reason, Vendor agrees not to provide similar software integration services to any competitor of Customer operating within North America, Europe, or the Asia-Pacific region.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA GOVERNANCE
Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of Proprietary Information using at least reasonable care. Vendor warrants that it complies with applicable data protection regulations including GDPR and CCPA, and shall notify Customer within twenty-four (24) hours of any suspected unauthorized access to personal data.

7. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to conflict of laws principles. Any legal suit, action, or proceeding shall be instituted exclusively in the federal or state courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.
